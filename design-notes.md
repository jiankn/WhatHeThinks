# Design notes

## Product and screen job

WhatHeThinks is a relationship text analyzer for people who want evidence about effort, reply behavior, and changes over time. The homepage's primary job is to get a visitor to start a free chat analysis; the secondary job is to make the paid report concrete before they begin.

## Directions and decisions

- 2026-09-18: The sunset editorial direction replaced an earlier scrolling chat-wall concept. Do not re-propose the chat wall by default.
- 2026-09-19: Homepage direction refined to **Private relationship field note**. Photography carries intimacy in the hero; an interactive sample report carries trust and product specificity.
- Existing sunset photography, warm paper, plum, and rose brand colors were retained. No new generated media was needed.
- The four-icon feature grid, standalone platform section, second lifestyle photo, avatar question cards, and flower CTA were removed. Their jobs are now handled by the interactive evidence sample, compact import path, question list, and pricing/privacy close.

## Critic loop

Project instructions prohibit unapproved subagents, so these reviews use the AI Design Studio cold-read fallback and are less independent than a fresh screenshot-only critic.

### Iteration 1 — 8.2/10

- Aesthetic read: intimate editorial relationship journal paired with an evidence-led product report; warmer and more human than conventional SaaS.
- Keep: clean mobile hero, bold cream/plum contrast, report sample as the signature element.
- Addressed: mobile report tabs extended beyond the visible panel; changed them to three equal columns and added arrow-key navigation.
- Addressed: repeated uppercase labels above every later section read as a template tell; removed labels from process, questions, and pricing.
- Protected: accurate pricing, privacy wording, platform limitations, sample-data disclaimer, and the sunset photography direction.

### Iteration 2 — 8.8/10

- The desktop composition now has one clear visual peak in the hero and one functional peak in the sample report; later sections recede appropriately.
- The mobile page preserves the same hierarchy without placing copy over faces, and all three evidence tabs remain visible at 320px.
- Remaining limitation: this score comes from the same-session cold-read fallback. A fresh critic was not used because project instructions disallow unapproved subagents.
- Exit: the page clears the intended quality bar, the key usability gap is resolved, and further decorative changes would work against the subtraction pass.

### Copy correction

- Replaced “Don’t take the conclusion on faith” with “See the evidence behind the conclusion.” The earlier idiom intended to promise transparency, but its opening could be read as telling customers not to trust the product.

## Media

All imagery is existing project media. No generation prompt was used in this revision.

## Final subtraction list

- Remove feature-icon grid: the sample report demonstrates the same value with evidence.
- Merge platform support into the three-step import path: platform choice is part of starting, not a separate product story.
- Remove avatar portraits from question links: they looked like testimonials and added no decision value.
- Remove secondary lifestyle and flower imagery: one strong photograph is enough; the report should own the rest of the page.
- Simplify the final section to price, privacy, and one action.
